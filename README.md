# 📈 Etsy Market & Data Patterns Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes **Etsy stock performance, market trends, trading activity, and sentiment data** to identify patterns and explore how data can support business and investment-related decision-making.

Using **Microsoft Excel and Power BI**, I analyzed two years of historical Etsy stock data and incorporated techniques including **moving averages, Bollinger Bands, forecasting, trend analysis, and sentiment analysis**.

The original project was expanded into an interactive **Power BI dashboard** that consolidates the key quantitative analyses into a single view, allowing users to explore Etsy's historical stock performance, market behavior, and sentiment patterns.

---

## 🎯 Analysis Objectives

The project explores several areas of Etsy's market and business performance:

- How did Etsy's stock price change over the historical period analyzed?
- What patterns are visible in Etsy's trading volume?
- How does Etsy's closing price compare with its moving average?
- What do Bollinger Bands reveal about stock-price movement and volatility?
- What trends can be identified through historical stock data?
- What does sentiment data indicate about public perception of Etsy?
- What might historical patterns suggest about future stock-price behavior?
- How can broader data patterns support business decision-making?

### Dashboard Analysis

The dashboard brings together several components of the analysis, including:

- **Stock Performance** — examines changes in Etsy's historical closing stock price and trading activity.
- **Trading Volume** — provides insight into the number of Etsy shares traded throughout the period analyzed.
- **Simple Moving Average (SMA)** — compares closing-price behavior with the underlying moving average to identify broader price trends.
- **Bollinger Bands** — evaluates stock-price movement relative to upper and lower statistical bands to visualize volatility and potential changes in market behavior.
- **Market Sentiment** — examines the distribution of positive, neutral, and negative sentiment.
- **Stock Price Forecast** — uses historical monthly closing-price patterns to generate a 12-month forecast and confidence interval.

## 🔎 Stock Market Analysis

### Historical Stock Performance

Two years of historical Etsy stock-market data were analyzed, including:

- Closing Price
- Opening Price
- Daily High
- Daily Low
- Trading Volume

The historical data was used to examine changes in Etsy's stock price and trading activity over time and served as the foundation for additional technical analysis.

### Simple Moving Average

A **Simple Moving Average (SMA)** was used to smooth short-term fluctuations in Etsy's stock price and provide a clearer view of the underlying trend.

Comparing the closing price with the moving average helps identify periods when the stock price moves above or below its recent historical trend.

### Bollinger Bands

Bollinger Bands were incorporated to analyze Etsy's stock-price movement and volatility.

The analysis includes:

- **Upper Bollinger Band**
- **Closing Price**
- **Simple Moving Average**
- **Lower Bollinger Band**

Together, these measures provide additional context for understanding how far the stock price moves from its recent average and how volatility changes over time.

---

## 🔮 Stock Price Forecast

A forecasting model was incorporated into the Power BI analysis to explore potential future stock-price behavior based on historical patterns.

To create a consistent time series for forecasting, daily historical stock observations were aggregated into **monthly periods**, using the **average closing price for each month**.

The resulting forecast extends the historical analysis by approximately **12 months** and includes a confidence interval to communicate the uncertainty associated with future estimates.

The forecast is intended as an analytical exploration of historical patterns rather than a prediction or investment recommendation.
